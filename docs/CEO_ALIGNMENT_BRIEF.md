# CEO Alignment Brief — AI-Powered Organic Growth

**Latest Craze Productions** · June 2026  
**Full narrative:** [CEO Executive Summary](./CEO_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md) · **Implementation:** [Product Implementation Plan](./PRODUCT_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)

---

## 1. What business problem are we solving?

Latest Craze Productions relies heavily on **outbound sales, referrals, repeat clients, and relationships**. That works — but lead generation scales with sales headcount, not with brand or search presence.

The website today is a **static marketing presence**. It does not systematically attract, convert, or measure qualified buyers from organic search.

---

## 2. What outcome are we targeting?

Build a **recurring inbound lead-generation engine** that produces enough **qualified opportunities** to support a **dedicated inbound sales organization** within **12–24 months**.

We are not optimizing for page count. We are optimizing for **leads → opportunities → pipeline → revenue**.

---

## 3. What are the two tracks?

| Track | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Track A — Demand Capture** | Rank for high-intent searches (event production, AV, LED walls); expand into priority cities; keep content fresh |
| **Track B — Authority & Demand** | Case studies, executive strategy content, conversion paths, planning tools, revenue funnel tracking |

Both tracks run on the **same automation engine**. Every change requires **human PR approval** before anything goes live.

---

## 4. What does each phase produce?

| Phase | Produces | Success signal |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **1 — National Foundation** | Nationwide hub, core services, first case studies & strategy content | National keyword visibility |
| **2 — Geographic Expansion** | 10 pilot cities with localized pages | City-level organic visibility |
| **3 — Scale** | Up to 100-market footprint, quality-controlled batches | Meaningful increase in organic opportunities |
| **4 — Operate** | Continuous refresh, conversion optimization, pruning | Sustainable inbound lead engine |

---

## 5. How will we measure success?

**Business outcomes — not publishing volume:**

| Layer | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Visibility** | Impressions, ranking keywords, geo coverage |
| **Traffic** | Organic sessions, priority-market traffic |
| **Leads** | Form submissions, consultations, quote requests |
| **Revenue** | Organic-sourced opportunities, pipeline, closed revenue |

Leadership sets funnel targets in the **first 30 days** (sessions → leads → opportunities → revenue). Monthly reporting compares actuals to those targets.

---

## 6. What decision does leadership need to make?

**Approve** an AI-powered organic growth program that:

1. Commits **~2–4 hours/week** for marketing PR review
2. Sets **revenue funnel targets** in month one
3. Authorizes **phased rollout:** national foundation → 10-city pilot → scale
4. Accepts **Cursor API cost model** (~1–2 automated runs/week; bulk builds manual)
