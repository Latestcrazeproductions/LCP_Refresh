# Corporate Gala Production Guide

Corporate galas demand flawless AV, staging, and guest flow. This guide covers what planners should prioritize when hiring a production partner.

## Define the guest experience first

Start with how attendees should feel walking in — not which gear you need. Production choices follow experience goals.

## Align AV with venue constraints

Load-in windows, power, rigging points, and noise ordinances shape what's possible. Confirm these before finalizing creative.

## Build in rehearsal time

General session and awards segments need dedicated rehearsal — not just a sound check.
