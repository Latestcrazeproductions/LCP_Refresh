# Corporate Event Production Checklist

Use this checklist when planning corporate conferences, galas, and general sessions.

## 8–12 weeks out

- Confirm venue load-in / load-out windows
- Define general session run-of-show
- Identify AV power and rigging requirements

## 4–6 weeks out

- Finalize staging footprint and sight lines
- Confirm IMAG and presentation flow
- Schedule full cue-to-cue rehearsal

## 1 week out

- Walk all cues with show caller
- Confirm backup plans for critical systems
